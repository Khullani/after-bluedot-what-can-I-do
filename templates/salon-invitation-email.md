# Salon Invitation Email

> Recruiting senior practitioners. Send to one person at a time, personalized.
> Do not BCC five people on the same email — it signals you don't know them
> well enough to write a personal note.

---

**Subject:** [First name] — small group on AI governance, [start month]

---

Dear [First name],

I'm putting together a small reading group for senior practitioners in
[city/region] who are working at or near the intersection of AI and
[their field — e.g., state government, healthcare policy, financial
regulation].

The format is five 90-minute sessions, weekly, in person at
[neighborhood / venue]. We'd start the week of [date]. The reading
load is light — one paper or chapter per week, plus one local
artifact (a bill, a procurement, a news piece). We operate under
Chatham House Rules so people can speak candidly.

The curriculum is adapted from the BlueDot Impact AGI Strategy
course, which I facilitated earlier this year, with two adjustments
for our context: [1 sentence on the local angle].

I'm inviting [number] people — all senior practitioners in adjacent
fields, none of you currently work together as far as I know. I
wanted you specifically because [one specific reason — their
expertise, their seat, a conversation you had with them].

If you're game, I'll send the full reading list and the dates
within a week. If you're not, I'd appreciate a quick "no thanks" so
I can extend the slot to someone else — the group works only at
4–6 participants.

[Closing — see you soon / talk to you at the next [thing] / etc.]

[Your name]

---

## Notes on use

- Personalize the **last paragraph above the close**. The reason you
  invited *them* is what makes this email work. Skip that paragraph and
  it reads like a mass mailer.
- Send 1–2 emails a day max, with personal notes. Eight emails over
  one week beats eight emails sent at the same time.
- Confirm with two informal yeses before you send the formal invites
  so you can name the others. "I have soft yeses from [X] and [Y]"
  is a strong tailwind.
- Follow up once after 5 business days. After that, treat silence as
  no.
