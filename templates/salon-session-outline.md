# Salon Session — 90-Minute Outline

> Use this for each of your 4–6 weekly sessions. Adapt as the cohort settles.

**Session:** [N of N]
**Topic:** [Topic for the week]
**Pre-read:** [1 paper or chapter + 1 local artifact]

---

## 0:00 – 0:10 — Settle-in and check-in

Coffee, hellos, brief check-in question that ties to the topic.

> Example check-in for session 1: "In one sentence, what brought you to
> this group?"

## 0:10 – 0:25 — Pre-read recap (participant-led)

Pick one participant in advance to summarize the pre-read in 5 minutes,
then a 10-minute open discussion of points that landed or didn't.

The facilitator's job here is to *not* re-explain the paper. The
participants did the reading.

## 0:25 – 0:55 — Tabletop exercise or scenario

A 30-minute structured exercise that applies the week's concept to a
concrete scenario in the participants' field.

> Examples:
> - "A state agency in [your jurisdiction] is procuring a model for
>   eligibility decisions. Walk through the procurement, the risks, the
>   safeguards you'd require."
> - "A reporter calls you tonight asking [question]. What do you say?"
> - "Your director asks for a 1-page memo by Monday on [topic]. Draft
>   the TL;DR together."

The tabletop is the part that separates this from a reading group.
Don't skip it.

## 0:55 – 1:15 — Open discussion

Now-unstructured discussion grounded in the tabletop. Participants will
naturally bring in their institutional context. The facilitator's job
is to draw out the quiet voices, not to steer.

> Useful prompts:
> - "[Name], you didn't get to speak in the tabletop — what's your read?"
> - "Where does this break down in your context?"
> - "What's the strongest objection to what we just said?"

## 1:15 – 1:25 — Action commitments

Each participant names one concrete thing they'll do between now and
next session — a memo they'll write, a meeting they'll take, a paper
they'll send.

Write these down. Open the next session with a quick check on whether
they happened.

## 1:25 – 1:30 — Closing and next week

Confirm next week's pre-read and the discussion-leader for it. Pick a
different participant each week.

---

## Facilitator notes

- **Plan 3–5 hours of prep per session**, especially the first time
  through any of the material. It gets faster after session 2.
- **Rotate the discussion-leader role.** Forces engagement; spreads
  ownership.
- **Don't fill silence.** When you ask a question and the room is
  quiet, count to ten before rephrasing. The quiet voices speak
  during silence.
- **Track who's spoken.** Aim for everyone to speak in each segment.
  If somebody hasn't, call on them gently.
- **Send a 5-line recap by email within 24 hours.** Three takeaways,
  the action commitments, the pre-read for next week. Compounds the
  cohort's memory.
