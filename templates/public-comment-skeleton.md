## Introduction (1–2 paragraphs)

Identify yourself briefly. State your position in the second sentence.
Don't bury it. Don't open with three paragraphs of "we appreciate the
agency's thoughtful consideration."

> Example: "I am [name], [affiliation/independent], based in [city]. I
> facilitate the BlueDot Impact Frontier AI Governance course locally and
> have worked with [N] practitioners on AI governance projects. I write to
> recommend that [agency] [do X / not do Y / clarify Z]."

[2–4 paragraphs. Substantive. Cite evidence. Take a clear position. End the
section with a one-sentence recommendation: "We recommend that [agency]
…"]

## Question 2 of [N]: [Quote next question]

[Same structure. 2–4 paragraphs, evidence, recommendation.]

## Question 3 of [N]: [Quote next question]

## Pre-submit checklist

- [ ] Position stated in the first or second sentence.
- [ ] 2–4 specific questions addressed, not all of them.
- [ ] Each section ends with a one-sentence recommendation.
- [ ] Numbered summary at the end.
- [ ] Citations real and live.
- [ ] Submitted at least 3 days before the deadline. (Buffers exist for a
      reason.)
- [ ] Receipt / docket entry saved for your records.
