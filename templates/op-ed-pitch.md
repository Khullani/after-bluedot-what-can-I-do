# Op-Ed Pitch Email

> Send this *before* you draft the piece. 150–250 words. Subject line
> matters as much as the body.

---

**Subject:** Pitch: [6–8 word hook] — [Your name]

> Examples:
> - "Pitch: Illinois' new AI eligibility law is missing one paragraph — Khullani Abdullahi"
> - "Pitch: Why NIST's draft framework will fail in healthcare — Khullani Abdullahi"

---

**Body:**

Dear [Section editor's first name],

[1 sentence on the timely hook — what just happened, why this week.]

I'd like to pitch a 750-word op-ed arguing that [your one-sentence claim,
sharp and specific]. The piece would [1 sentence on what the body of the
argument looks like — the three beats, not the conclusions].

[1 sentence acknowledging the strongest counterargument and how the
piece addresses it. This is what makes a pitch land — it shows you're
not writing pure advocacy.]

Why me: [2 lines on your credibility for this piece. Not your CV — the
specific things that make you qualified for *this* argument. If you
facilitated a BlueDot course, ran a salon, wrote a related landscape doc,
name it.]

The piece is timely because [specific reason it has to run this week or
next — pending vote, pending rulemaking, news cycle]. I can have a draft
to you within [N] days of confirmation.

Happy to send the draft on spec if that's easier. Either way, thanks for
considering.

Best,
[Your name]
[One-line bio: affiliation or independent + city]
[Email · phone optional]

---

## Follow-up rules

- Wait 5 business days. If no reply, send a one-line bump: "Following
  up on the pitch below — let me know if it's a fit."
- Wait 5 more business days. If still no reply, withdraw and pitch to
  the next outlet on your list. Do not let a piece die in one editor's
  inbox.
