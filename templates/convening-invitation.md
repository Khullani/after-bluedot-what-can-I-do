# Convening Invitation Email

> Personalized to each invitee. Send 10 invites to get 8 confirmed.

---

**Subject:** Working session on [topic], [date] — small group

---

Dear [First name],

I'm hosting a small working session on [specific topic] on [day, date]
from [start] to [end], at [venue, neighborhood]. It will be eight
people, Chatham House Rules.

We'll spend 90 minutes producing [the output — a one-page memo / a
shared question set / a working agreement]. There's a 5-page pre-read
I'll circulate a week ahead.

The other invitees include [3–4 names you can name — confirmed ones
are a strong signal, but pending is fine too if you say so]. I'd like
you specifically because [one specific reason tied to their expertise
or their seat].

Could you let me know by [date — 5 business days out] whether you can
make it? If yes, I'll send the pre-read and venue logistics by [date].

[Close]

[Your name]

---

## Notes on use

- **Lock the output in the second paragraph.** Without it, senior
  people read this as "a coffee chat with extra steps" and politely
  decline.
- **Name confirmed attendees** when you have 2–3. Social proof is the
  difference between yes and "let me check my calendar."
- **Set a real RSVP deadline** so you can replace declines.
- **Send the pre-read 5–7 days ahead**, not earlier. Earlier and they
  forget; later and they don't read it.
