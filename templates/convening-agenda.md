# Convening Agenda — 90-Minute Working Session

**Topic:** [Specific topic]
**Date / time / venue:** [Specifics]
**Output:** [The artifact you will produce together by the end]
**Pre-read:** [Circulated 5–7 days ahead]
**Ground rules:** Chatham House (default for senior participants)

---

## 0:00 – 0:10 — Welcome and framing

The host (you) opens with:

- Why this group, in three sentences.
- The output for the session, named again. ("By 5:30, we'll have a
  one-page memo we all agree with — or have surfaced where we
  disagree.")
- A one-sentence round: each person says their name, role, and one
  thing they hope to get out of the session.

Keep this tight. Don't let it eat 25 minutes.

## 0:10 – 0:30 — Pre-read positions

Each person, in 2 minutes, gives their position on the pre-read.
Round-robin. No back-and-forth yet.

> Prompt: "What did you agree with, what did you push back on, what
> question do you wish the pre-read had answered?"

The facilitator captures positions on a shared doc or whiteboard.

## 0:30 – 1:00 — Structured working block

This is where the output gets built. Pick one of:

**Option A — Drafting block.** The group drafts the output (a memo, a
question set, a recommendation) together in a shared document, with the
facilitator typing. Stop when the doc is roughly there or when the room
has clearly stalled.

**Option B — Tabletop scenario.** A specific scenario you've prepared:
"A reporter calls Friday asking [question]. What do we say?" / "The
governor's office asks for a position by Monday. What's our advice?"

**Option C — Disagreement mapping.** Where do we disagree, why, and on
what evidence would we change our minds? The output is the map of
disagreement, which is itself valuable.

Pick one. Don't try all three.

## 1:00 – 1:20 — Output refinement

Read the draft output out loud. Each participant flags one change they
want. Make changes live. Don't ratify what isn't agreed.

## 1:20 – 1:30 — Next steps

Each participant names one thing they'll do off the back of this
session — a person they'll talk to, a follow-up memo they'll send, a
public comment they'll file.

Confirm the host (you) will circulate the final output and the
commitments within 48 hours.

---

## Within 48 hours after

Send a single email to all participants with:

1. The final output (memo, question set, etc).
2. The list of commitments each person named (use only first names if
   the group prefers).
3. The next step — "I'll convene us again in [N] months if there's
   appetite. Reply to this email if you'd come back."

This email is the artifact that compounds. Without it, the convening
fades by Monday.

---

## Facilitator notes

- **Eight is plenty.** Above twelve, people perform rather than think.
- **The output design is more important than the agenda.** If the
  output is fuzzy, the session is fuzzy.
- **Time the segments visibly.** A printed agenda on the table, with
  times, keeps the room honest.
- **End on time, every time.** Senior people return to events that
  respect their calendar.
