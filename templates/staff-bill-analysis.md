## When to use this variant vs. the 2-page recommendation memo

Use the **staff bill analysis** (this template) when:
- The audience is committee staff or members who need to understand the
  bill as a whole before they can act on a recommendation.
- The bill is complex enough that a 2-page recommendation memo would
  leave too many open questions about what's actually in it.
- You want the analysis to outlive the immediate vote — staff analyses
  get filed and re-read in subsequent sessions.

Use the **2-page recommendation memo** ([`one-pager.md`](./one-pager.md))
when:
- The audience is one decision-maker who already understands the bill.
- The ask is binary or narrow (vote yes / vote no / amend this line).
- You need to fit on a single screen of a phone.
