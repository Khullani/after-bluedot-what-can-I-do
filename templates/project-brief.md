name when possible.

## The gap this fills

sentence starts with "there's not enough writing on…" search again — there

## The v1 deliverable

memo," "a website with bill listings and TL;DRs," "a 60-minute convening
with 8 named participants."

## The success signal

How will you know v1 worked? One sentence. Examples:

- "The chief of staff replied."
- "A reporter cited the tracker."
- "Two participants asked when the next salon is."
- "The op-ed ran."
