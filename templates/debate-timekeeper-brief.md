# Public Debate — Timekeeper Brief

**Your job:** keep every speaker to the clock with three cards, and give a
clean hard stop. You hold the cards; the moderator handles any spoken
intervention.

## The three cards (same rule for every speech)

| Card | Color | Raise when |
|---|---|---|
| **1 MIN** | Amber | 1:00 remains |
| **30 SEC** | Orange | 0:30 remains |
| **TIME** | Red | 0:00 — plus a soft chime |

Hold each card at the speaker's eye level for ~5 seconds, then lower it. Keep
**TIME** raised until they actually stop.

## What's timed (fill in your order)

| Segment | Limit | Speaking order |
|---|---|---|
| Opening statements | [6:00] each | [order] |
| Rebuttals | [3:00] each | [order] |
| Closing statements | [2:00] each | [order] |

## Running each speech

- Start the countdown on the speaker's **first word**, not during the
  moderator's introduction.
- Raise 1 MIN → 30 SEC → TIME at the marks. Stop and reset for the next
  speaker.
- If a speaker runs past TIME, keep TIME up. After ~10–15 seconds the
  moderator will wrap them verbally; you stay silent.
- Cross-examination and audience Q&A are run by the moderator. You are off the
  clock unless asked to flag a two-minute mark.

## Your kit and setup (before doors)

- Countdown timer on a laptop or tablet — test it and know how to reset it.
- The three printed cards, stacked in order, within reach.
- A soft chime or bell for the hard stop only (no sound at the warnings).
- A clear sightline to both lecterns and the moderator; agree on a quick
  eye-contact signal.

*Print the cards one per landscape sheet on cardstock; laminate them if you
can.*
