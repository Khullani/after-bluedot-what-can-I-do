# Public Debate — Communications Suite

Fill the `[BRACKETS]`. The order below roughly follows the event timeline.

---

## 1. Sponsor pitch (pitch the *series*, not one event)

**Subject:** Backing a year of serious AI-safety conversation in [CITY]

Hi [NAME],

[WARM INTRO / how you're connected.] I'm building an ongoing series of public
AI-safety events in [CITY] — debates and conversations that bring serious,
good-faith disagreement about AI into the same room. The first is [EVENT +
DATE].

I'd love [ORG] as a founding partner for the series rather than a single night.
It's a cleaner story for you and a steadier platform for us. Support would
cover [venue / travel / catering], and in return you'd get [logo placement,
remarks, a reserved block of seats, first look at future events].

Could we find 20 minutes this week?

Best,

[YOU]

---

## 2. Debater invite (be specific about the support you can offer)

**Subject:** Invitation: [MOTION] — [CITY], [DATE]

Hi [NAME],

I'm organizing an Oxford-style debate on the motion *"[MOTION]"* in [CITY] on
[DATE], and I'd be honored to have you argue [FOR / AGAINST]. It's a serious,
good-faith format — two speakers per side, a moderated cross-examination, and
a before/after audience vote — with an engaged crowd of [~N].

The commitment is one evening plus light prep. [TRAVEL SUPPORT: We can cover
your flight and two nights' hotel / We have up to $X available for travel / We
cannot cover travel.] [HONORARIUM: We can offer $X / This is an unpaid event.]

Would you be up for it? Happy to send the full format and the other confirmed
debaters.

Best,

[YOU]

## 3. Venue confirmation (lock logistics in writing, ~2 weeks out)

Confirming the essentials and the flow so AV and timing are easy to follow.

**Essentials:** [DATE] · [VENUE] · doors [TIME] · program [TIME] · reception
[TIME] · ~[N] expected.

**A favor:** would [HOST] open the evening on behalf of [VENUE] — a warm

**Run of show:** [paste the segment list.]

## 5. Thank-you to attendees (includes a missed-you line)

**Subject:** Thank you — and what's next for AI safety in [CITY]

Hi everyone,

What a night. Thank you for filling the room and bringing exactly the kind of
engaged, good-faith curiosity that makes a real debate work. This wasn't
believers versus dismissers — it was serious people who share a concern about
AI and reach different conclusions about what to do.

And the room moved. [One line on the vote shift.]

If [weather / anything] kept you from making it, you were missed. The good
news is this was the first of many, with plenty more chances to be in the room
ahead.

None of it happens without [VENUE], [SPONSOR], and our debaters — [NAMES +
AFFILIATIONS] — who made their cases with real rigor and good humor.

A few places to go deeper: [3–5 linked resources].

Two quick asks (about three minutes): tell us what you'd like to see next — and
if you're hiring, looking for a role, or building something that needs support,
this is how we connect the dots → [FORM LINK].

Stay in touch: [community list / event series / LinkedIn].

Warmly,

[YOU]

**Delivery note:** send through the event platform, a compliant mailing list,
or BCC. Do not expose attendee email addresses, and name individual attendees
only with their consent.

## 8. Post-event intake form invite

The attendee email's call to action can carry this. Keep the form under about
three minutes and include sections for:

- future events people want;
- who's hiring, open to roles, or between organizations;
- who's building something that needs funding or collaborators; and
- a clear community opt-in.
