# Public Debate — Moderator Run of Show

*Italic text is spoken; plain text is an action. Times are targets; the
timekeeper holds the speeches to the clock.*

| Time | Segment | Who | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome | Venue host | *Host opens on behalf of the venue (~2 min), then hands to you.* |
| 0:02 | Open the program | Moderator | *"Tonight isn't believers versus dismissers — it's serious people who take AI risk seriously and disagree about what to do."* State the motion; note that it is deliberately open. Give the one-line format and ground rules (no interruptions; state the event's photography/recording policy). |
| 0:06 | Introduce the debaters | Moderator or timekeeper | Give brief bios for both sides. |
| 0:08 | **Opening vote** | Moderator + AV | *"Before a word of argument — where does the room stand? Scan the code and vote."* Lock after ~30 seconds; hide results. |
| 0:10 | Opening statements — 6 min each | Debaters | *"[Name], you have six minutes — the floor is yours."* Alternate sides. Call the next speaker without editorializing. |
| ~0:34 | Rebuttals — 3 min each | Debaters | *"Now three-minute rebuttals — respond to what you've heard."* |
| ~0:46 | Moderated cross-examination | Moderator | *"I'd like to put a few questions to the panel."* Stay neutral; steer with questions; *"[A], [B] — state the other side's strongest point."* Name the dodge when someone evades. |
| ~0:56 | Audience Q&A | Mic runner | *"We'll go to the floor — keep it to a question, and tell us who it's for."* Redirect speeches to questions; keep it balanced. |
| ~1:11 | Closing statements — 2 min each | Debaters | *"Two-minute closings, same order — [Name]."* |
| ~1:19 | **Final vote + reveal** | Moderator + AV | *"Now you've heard the case — vote once more."* Compare the results in the tool, reveal them sequentially, or use a prepared comparison slide: *"Here's where we started — and here's where we landed."* |
| ~1:23 | Close | Moderator | Thank debaters by name and affiliation, the venue, sponsors, and the room. Invite attendees to the reception or next step. |

**Moderator discipline:** neutrality above all; steer with questions, not
opinions; steel-man both sides; hold the motion open; keep applause and timing
tight.
