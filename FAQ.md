# FAQ

Short answers to the questions cohort members and facilitators ask most.
For the full reasoning behind any of these, the [`PLAYBOOK.md`](./PLAYBOOK.md)
is the long version.

## Using the playbook

**I just finished a course and I'm a bit overwhelmed. Where do I actually start?**
Read the [TL;DR in the README](./README.md), score yourself on the 0–10 clarity
question at the top of [`PLAYBOOK.md`](./PLAYBOOK.md), then pick **one** archetype
from the menu and commit to a v1 in six weeks. Don't read everything first.
The point is to start one finishable thing this week, not to study the menu.

**What if I scored myself a 0–3 on the clarity question?**
Then you're not picking from the menu yet — you're exploring. Do a short
conviction-building sprint first: 4–8 weeks of structured reading, written
reflections, and 3–5 conversations with people who've made the move you're
considering. Pick an archetype *after* that, not during. This is in the
playbook under "where are you, honestly?"

**I can only spare a few hours a week. Is any of this realistic?**
Yes — pick the right archetype for that constraint. Under ~4 hours/week, do
**#6 (public comment)** if there's an open window in your area, or **#3 (policy
memo)** if there isn't. Do **not** start a salon or a tracker on three-hour
weeks; that's the single most common way people quit at week four feeling
worse than when they started.

**Do I really have to pick only one?**
For your first v1, yes. One. People who pick two finish zero. Once you've
shipped a v1, the playbook lists [combinations that pair well](./PLAYBOOK.md)
(memo → op-ed, landscape doc → convening, and so on).

**Do I have to write publicly? That feels self-promotional.**
You don't have to, but the cohort members who paired an archetype with
consistent public writing — even a Substack with twelve subscribers — have
without exception gotten more interesting opportunities than the ones who
didn't. The bar is low: 400 words a week, posted as-is. It's a thinking and
discovery tool, not a personal brand. See "Write while you do" in the playbook.

**There's no example for archetype #3 (policy memo). Why?**
Because good memos and staff bill analyses are usually private — they're live
through active negotiation, so publishing them would burn the relationship that
made them useful. The absence is honest, not an oversight. Use the
[`one-pager.md`](./templates/one-pager.md) and
[`staff-bill-analysis.md`](./templates/staff-bill-analysis.md) templates instead.

## Scope and fit

**Do I have to be a BlueDot alum to use this?**
No. It's written as a hand-off for alumni of the BlueDot Frontier AI Governance
course because that's who I facilitate, but the menu works for anyone leaving a
structured AI-policy program with momentum and an empty calendar. Use it.

**Can I use this for a field that isn't AI governance?**
The archetypes (tracker, memo, op-ed, convening, etc.) are general-purpose moves
for turning study into impact. The examples and framing are AI-governance-specific,
but the structure ports cleanly to adjacent policy fields. Fork it and adapt the
examples to your domain.

**Which archetype is "best"?**
There isn't one. The best archetype is the one that matches where you already
have credibility, the time you actually have, and a specific named human who'll
read what you produce. Those are the "three questions" in the playbook. Picking
the most prestigious-sounding archetype instead of the best-fit one is the most
common mistake.

## Contributing and reuse

**Can I reuse this for my own cohort?**
Yes — that's the point. It's licensed [CC BY 4.0](./LICENSE): fork it, adapt it,
run it for your own people. Attribution appreciated, not required. If you're
handing it off, start with [`FACILITATING.md`](./FACILITATING.md).

**I'm a facilitator. Where do I start?**
[`FACILITATING.md`](./FACILITATING.md) — when to send the playbook, the
closing-note template, how to run office hours, and how to log what your cohort
shipped. Then add yourself to the facilitators list and open a cohort-entry
issue (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md)).

**Can I add my own example or propose a new archetype?**
Please do — that's how the menu compounds. Shipped artifacts are the
highest-value contribution. New archetypes have a higher bar: propose only what
you've watched at least one person actually ship. Full details and the voice
contract are in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Is there a live community — office hours, WhatsApp, Slack?**
Not attached to this repo, no. The maintainer doesn't currently run a
cross-facilitator office hours series or chat group.
[`COMMUNITY.md`](./COMMUNITY.md) has patterns (cadence, agenda, norms) you can
adapt if you want to set one up around your own cohort or local network. For
direct contact with the maintainer: open an issue, open a PR, or email
**info@techne.ai**.

**Why is the playbook so opinionated and first-person?**
On purpose. A hand-off works when someone tells you what to do this week, not
when it hands you a balanced catalog to deliberate over. The
[voice contract in `CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how that voice
stays coherent as other people contribute.
