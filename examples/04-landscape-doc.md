# Example — Landscape doc

> Archetype #4.

## The artifact

**The inaugural white paper from the [AI Safety Chicago](https://luma.com/ais-chicago)
event series**, co-hosted by Techne AI and BlueDot Impact.

- **Link:** [Google Doc — AI Safety Chicago white paper](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1w-KqzvdN-GqH6pA4I0koqYnKEySwKHtS_945Bgp-fXk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.2gsm4b1fvo18)
- **Status:** Draft.
- **Genre:** Convening-driven landscape document — the kind of doc that
  uses a structured event as its pressure-test before publication.

## Why this is a useful example for the archetype

- **It's a convening output, not a solo desk-research project.** The
  doc has the texture of a landscape paper but earns that texture
  through 120 practitioners' positions surfaced at the event, rather
  than one author's reading list. That makes the scope honest and the
  findings sharper.
- **It pairs with a recurring series.** A landscape doc with a planned
  v2 — drawn from event #2 — is structurally more durable than a
  one-shot publication. The next event becomes the pressure test, and
  the next paper becomes the artifact. The compounding starts on day
  one.
- **Cross-sector mix in the source pool.** The event audience (Chicago
  universities + regulatory/legal practitioners) means the paper isn't
  just researchers talking to researchers. That's exactly the
  "under-covered angle" framing archetype #4 calls for — a topic where
  several adjacent literatures exist but the synthesis between them
  doesn't.

## The combination it demonstrates

This is the **archetype #7 (convening) → archetype #4 (landscape doc)**
move named in [`PLAYBOOK.md`](../PLAYBOOK.md#combinations-that-work).
Use the convening to gather positions; use the doc to make those
positions legible and citable; use the doc as the pre-read for the next
convening.

## What I'd do differently next time

- Publish a v1 *before* the second event, not after — so event #2 can
  react to a public draft rather than waiting on a private one.
- Add a one-page executive summary that's separable from the long doc,
  so journalists and staff can pull from it without reading 6,000 words.
- Maintain a changelog so cited versions are stable.
