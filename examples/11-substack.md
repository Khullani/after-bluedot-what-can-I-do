# Example — Substack

> Archetype #11.

## The artifact

**"Parrhesia, Praxis, and Poetry: AI Governance's Truth-Telling
Problem"** — a post on the [Heterotopia AI](https://heterotopiaai.substack.com/)
Substack, published immediately after the author finished a BlueDot
course (Frontier AI Governance).

- **Link:** [heterotopiaai.substack.com/p/parrhesia-praxis-and-poetry-ai-governances](https://heterotopiaai.substack.com/p/parrhesia-praxis-and-poetry-ai-governances)
- **Status:** Published.
- **Genre:** Course-reflection post — uses the discipline of writing to
  consolidate what the course actually changed in the author's
  thinking.

## Why this is a useful example for the archetype

- **It uses the post to think, not to perform.** The writing is
  exploratory in places, taking risks of position rather than presenting
  a polished thesis. That's exactly what makes the four-week, four-post
  discipline useful: posts that try things land more honestly than ones
  that polish.
- **It's specifically post-course.** Most post-course writing tries to
  sound smart about AI governance generally. This post takes one
  specific angle — parrhesia, the classical Greek practice of frank
  truth-telling — from the course and pushes on it. A specific angle
  beats a survey.
- **It demonstrates the four-post discipline can begin anywhere.** No
  grand opening manifesto. Just a question the course raised, pursued
  in writing.
