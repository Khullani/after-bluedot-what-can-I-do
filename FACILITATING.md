# Facilitating: handing this playbook off to a cohort

This is the facilitator's entry point. If you just finished a course and you're
deciding what to do next, you want [`PLAYBOOK.md`](./PLAYBOOK.md), not this file —
this one is about handing that playbook to *other* people.

## What you're handing off

The playbook solves one specific failure: the quiet six weeks after a course
ends, when the worldview is fresh, the calendar is empty, and almost nobody
ships anything. Your job as the person handing it off is to convert that
momentum into one finishable commitment per participant before it evaporates.
The menu does the work; you provide the timing, the nudge, and the
accountability.

## The hand-off, step by step

1. **Send the closing note 1–2 days after the last session.** Not a week later —
   the momentum has a short half-life. Use
   [`templates/facilitator-wrap-up-note.md`](./templates/facilitator-wrap-up-note.md)
   as the scaffold. The playbook goes *inside* that note as the main content,
   not as a separate "here's a resource" link people will never open.

2. **Ask the two clarity questions in the note.** "On a scale of 0–10, how clear
   are you on your next steps?" and "What would help move you closer to a 10?"
   These do more work than the menu itself — they tell each person whether
   they're ready to pick or still need an exploration sprint.

3. **Ask each person to pick one archetype and say it out loud** in the cohort
   channel. Public commitment is the mechanism. One archetype, one v1, six weeks.
   Watch for the person quietly picking two — redirect them to one.

4. **Follow up at ~two weeks.** A single "how's the v1 going?" in the channel
   catches the people who stalled while it's still cheap to restart. This is the
   highest-leverage five minutes you'll spend.

5. **Log what happened** in [`CHANGELOG.md`](./CHANGELOG.md) once the dust
   settles — who picked what, what shipped, what stalled. The "what stalled"
   notes are as useful to the next facilitator as the wins.

## A few things I've learned running this

- **The clarity question beats the menu.** People who can't answer question 2
  honestly will bounce off any archetype. Spend your attention there first.
- **Name the human.** The most common reason a v1 dies is that the participant
  never had a specific reader in mind. Push every person to name the one human
  whose decision, attention, or invitation they're aiming at.
- **Protect the failure-mode sections.** When you adapt the playbook for your
  cohort, keep the "bad if…" parts intact. They're what stops people picking the
  prestigious-sounding archetype over the right-fit one.
- **One follow-up is worth ten reminders up front.** Front-loaded enthusiasm
  fades; a timed check-in at week two is what actually moves completion rates.

## After the cohort

- **Add what shipped to [`examples/`](./examples/).** A real artifact from your
  cohort is more useful to the next one than anything I can write. See
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the shape.
- **Add a cohort entry to [`CHANGELOG.md`](./CHANGELOG.md).** Course name, dates,
  participant count, what each person picked, what shipped.
- **Add yourself to the facilitators list** in [`README.md`](./README.md).

## Staying connected to other facilitators

[`COMMUNITY.md`](./COMMUNITY.md) is the hub:

- **Office hours** — monthly for the first three months after launch, quarterly
  after that. Trade what's working across cohorts and pressure-test new
  archetype ideas.
- **WhatsApp** — faster-than-email channel for facilitators running this
  playbook.
- **The lightest path in** — open a cohort-entry issue, request the WhatsApp
  invite, sign up for the next call.
